# Privacy Policy — Swalef (سوالف)

**Last updated: 12 September 2026**

---

## English

### Overview

Swalef does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app works entirely on your device and does not connect to the internet.

### Information we collect

**None.** Swalef does not collect any information about you or your device. Specifically:

- We do not collect names, email addresses, or any contact information
- We do not collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or location data
- We do not collect analytics, usage statistics, or crash reports
- We do not use cookies, trackers, or third-party analytics services
- The app has no user accounts and requires no sign-in

### Information stored on your device

Swalef saves a small amount of information locally on your device so the app works the way you expect:

- Player names you enter, so you don't have to retype them each session
- Your sound and haptics preferences
- Whether you have viewed each game's tutorial

This information never leaves your device. It is not transmitted anywhere, and we have no access to it. Deleting the app removes it permanently.

### Internet access

Swalef does not use the internet. The app functions fully offline and makes no network requests of any kind.

### Third-party services

Swalef contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics services.

### Children's privacy

Swalef does not collect data from anyone, including children. No personal information is gathered regardless of the user's age.

### Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds features that require data collection — this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. Any such change would be described here before it takes effect.

### Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: **swalef.app@gmail.com**
